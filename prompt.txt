THE GAME — HIVEWARDEN (original concept, no bee game in the top 10):
You are the guardian bee of a golden hive in a bright meadow. Top-down 2D canvas, no library, no network, synthesized WebAudio only, M mute, P pause.
- Steer the guardian bee (mouse and/or WASD/arrows). Sting enemies (click/space). Collect nectar from flowers to grow honey. Build wax walls (E) to block enemies. Call a swarm (Q). Pheromone burst (Shift).
- 7 named days with increasing waves: Day 1 Dawn Meadow, Day 2 Clover Field, Day 3 Poppy Row, Day 4 Sunflower Stand, Day 5 Berry Thicket, Day 6 Storm Meadow, Day 7 Dusk Bloom — BOSS: the Hornet Queen.
- Enemies (named, with counts in the THREAT QUEUE): Wasp, Mite, Spider, Beetle, Hornet. Each behaves differently (wasps fly fast, mites swarm, spiders slow, beetles tanky).
- Resources: NECTAR, POLLEN, HONEY (numbers in HUD). Relics (named, e.g. Royal Jelly, Amber Comb, Golden Pollen, Queen's Crown, Sunpetal). Abilities (named slots: Sting, Swarm Call, Wax Wall, Pheromone Burst, Nectar Dash, Royal Guard).
- Win: survive all 7 days and defeat the Hornet Queen → WIN screen with score + time bonus + PLAY AGAIN. Lose: hive health reaches 0 → withered screen with restart.
- HUD must include: top-left "DAY n/7 · <day name>" + boss line + hive health + flowers standing; top-center countdown + WIN/LOSE spelled out; top-right SCORE + BEST + COMBO + INCOMING (next wave timer) + upgrade list; right panel THREAT QUEUE (named enemies + counts) + NEXT wave; bottom resource bars + 6 named ability slots + PEAK/COMBO + GRADE; controls bar. Fill the corners — do not leave the HUD sparse.
- Scene must be DENSE and COLORFUL: green meadow with texture, many colorful flowers (red poppies, yellow sunflowers, purple clover, white daisies), the golden hive, dirt paths, a pond, bushes, bees, enemies, particles (pollen sparkles, sting flashes). Bright palette — no black background.
